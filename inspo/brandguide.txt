Contrast rules
Body text needs 4.5:1 contrast. Large or bold text (about 19px bold or 24px regular and up) needs 3:1.

Logo colours: pink tile, plum glasses and mustache, salmon nose. The inverted version is a plum tile with pink glasses.
Rules:
• Keep space around the logo equal to at least a quarter of the tile's width.
• Don't put the transparent version on plum. The plum glasses disappear.
• Don't stretch, rotate, or recolour the glasses outside the palette.
• Below about 32px, use the icon alone, without the wordmark.
• Use the SVG files on the website so the logo stays sharp.
UI style
The Pink Pop look: a loud pink hero on top, then plum and grey blue sections below, with no boxed cards.

Section rhythm: pink hero, then a plum band, then a grey blue content section, then an indigo call to action, then a plum footer.
Pack pages: the card layout is retired. The candidates are Editorial (big numbers and rules on plum), Cheat sheet (label and content rows on grey blue), and Tabs (one section at a time). Still to decide.
Voice and tone
Larp is in on the joke: playful, confident, and a little cheeky, but never mean.
Taglines in use:
• Know ball in 5 minutes.
• Act like you know ball.
• Larp responsibly.
• Could you pass as a real fan?
How to write:
• Short sentences, like talking to a friend.
• Use the slang the audience uses ("know ball", "hot take", "run it back"), but don't overdo it.
• Joke about bluffing, never about the hobbies or the fans themselves.
• Name things plainly: packs, topics, vibe check, write a pack.
• Lowercase "larp" in the logo; "Larp" at the start of a sentence.
